Handling & storage

General guidance for keeping eggs in good condition.

The following is general good practice for handling eggs. It describes common practice rather than a certification of our own operation.

Rotate stock first in, first out

Use the oldest crates first and keep new deliveries separate so nothing sits at the back unnoticed.

Point 1

Keep them cool and dry

Store away from direct sunlight, heat sources and strong odours, and refrigerate where you have the capacity.

Point 2

Reject damaged crates

Check crates on arrival and set aside any cracked, leaking or soiled eggs rather than mixing them in.
