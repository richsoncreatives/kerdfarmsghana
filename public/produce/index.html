Freshness & handling

General good practice for produce.

These are widely accepted handling principles for fresh produce. They describe general practice, not a certification of our own operation.

Grade and clean early

Sort by size and quality, remove damaged units, and keep produce dry where dampness would shorten its life.

Point 1

Keep the cold chain

Move produce to a cool, shaded place quickly after harvest and avoid stacking that crushes lower layers.

Point 2

Transport without bruising

Use rigid crates rather than loose sacks where produce is delicate, and avoid overloading to save a trip.

Point 3

Sell in the order you bought

Rotate older stock to the front of the display and use it first, so nothing is lost at the back.

Point 4
