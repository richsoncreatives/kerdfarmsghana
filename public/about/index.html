About Kerd Farms

An agricultural enterprise built around three essentials.

Kerd Farms works across poultry and egg production, fruit and vegetable supply and distribution, and manure and fertilizer products. By bringing these services together under one brand, we aim to make essential agricultural products more accessible to households, businesses, growers and institutions.

Our identity

A more resilient food system, built one supply relationship at a time.

Kerd Farms is an agricultural enterprise focused on three essential areas of the food and farming value chain: poultry and egg production, fruit and vegetable supply and distribution, and manure and fertilizer products.

Working across all three means we can serve a customer who needs eggs on Monday, produce on Wednesday and soil inputs before the season starts — on one relationship rather than three.

Beyond commercial supply, we recognise the importance of nutrition, community engagement and responsible agricultural development. That is why our outreach work with schoolchildren sits alongside the commercial side of the business rather than apart from it.

This describes our identity and purpose. Company history, founding details and operating locations are published once confirmed by the business — we do not invent them.

Mission

To provide dependable agricultural products and supply solutions that nourish communities, support businesses, improve access to nutritious food, and contribute to sustainable agricultural development.

Vision

To become a trusted agricultural brand in Ghana and across Africa, recognised for reliable supply, product quality, responsible agricultural practices, innovation, and meaningful community impact.

Strategic objectives

What we are working towards.

  1. 01

    Supply quality eggs and poultry products to households, retailers, businesses and institutions.

  2. 02

    Strengthen the distribution of fruits and vegetables through reliable, customer-focused supply solutions.

  3. 03

    Develop and distribute manure and fertilizer products that support responsible soil management and agricultural productivity.

  4. 04

    Build long-term relationships with farmers, retailers, distributors and commercial buyers.

  5. 05

    Expand access to agricultural products through convenient digital ordering and responsive customer service.

  6. 06

    Promote nutrition awareness and support community outreach programmes focused on children’s wellbeing.

  7. 07

    Improve operational efficiency, product traceability and customer experience through technology.

  8. 08

    Pursue sustainable business growth and explore opportunities for expansion across Ghana and other African markets.
