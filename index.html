Our outreach team visits schools and communities to share free boiled eggs and pepper with children, alongside age-appropriate nutrition education for pupils, parents and caregivers.

How bulk ordering works

  1. 01

    Tell us the requirement

    Products and categories, estimated quantities, delivery location and how often you need supply.

  2. 02

    We confirm availability

    You receive the products we can commit to for your dates, with current pricing for those volumes.

  3. 03

    Agree the arrangements

    Delivery scheduling, packaging format and payment method are agreed before the first dispatch.

  4. 04

    Ongoing supply

    Once the arrangement works, recurring orders can be placed directly by telephone or WhatsApp.

Delivery regions, charges and minimum volumes are confirmed per arrangement — we do not publish delivery promises we cannot keep.
